2. Economy & Finance (Turkey)

There is a mixed picture on the fuel front: while diesel was raised, gasoline was discounted (BloombergHT). The tension on the oil side also reflected on logistics; the rise of tanker rates to levels "more expensive than launching a rocket into space" points to a new cost item in supply chains. In the corporate sector, the period of the facility provided to companies with capital loss or insolvency was extended, and advance payments of four electricity distribution companies were postponed — standing out as steps to ease cash flow.

It was announced that the trading volume in the Turkish stock market reached $1.5 billion and that the target is $2.35 billion; the first journey on the Halkalı-Kapıkule High-Speed Train Line took place. The social media ban for those under 15 stood out on the agenda.

3. Money & Currency

The most intense file of the weekend was the fund regulation: it is stated that the liquidation of 131 funds wiped out a 1.55 trillion lira bubble; the 9-article law was submitted to Parliament, and it was emphasized that no public resources would be used (Paranin Yonu). Kurtulmuş said they put "3 important insurances" into the law; the Fund Coordination Board held its fourth meeting (Doviz.com). DW Türkçe included an analysis on what will determine the economic cost of the fund crisis.

On the exchange rate side, while DXY prepares for the 100 test with Fed hawkishness and geopolitical risks, it is debated whether the dollar's "safe-haven premium has ended" (Investing TR Forex). The controlled rise in Dollar/TL continues, and it is wondered what the inflation report will say about the exchange rate. Borsa Istanbul closed the week with a rise; the relationships in the oil, bond, and gold triangle are becoming increasingly complex (Paranin Yonu).

4. Real Estate

The determination of the October rent increase ceiling created the parameter of the new period for both tenants and landlords. Abroad, it was reported that housing prices in London have started to decline and market balances have changed (Herbir Emlak). On the corporate side, Emlak Konut GYO published a buyback announcement, while Sinpaş's holiday home concept was introduced to business representatives, and the news of a giant project for the center of Izmir was given. The date of TOKİ's rental housing lottery draw was also among the agenda items.

The Ministry of Trade & Foreign Trade published its September data bulletin; in the export-import survey prepared in parallel, expectations were seen to diverge (Sabah). While September foreign trade figures were closely followed by the public, on a regional basis, Erzincan's August data were also announced. On the export front, Turkish tea provided 25 million dollars of revenue in nine months (CNN Türk); it was also among the notes reported that price movements in the commodity market left "sweet" gains for investors (Habertürk).

6. Geopolitical & Global Economy

The most talked-about development of the weekend was Trump, who met with Putin, announcing that the US would buy tons of diesel from Russia (DW Türkçe); Zelensky described this agreement as a gift to Putin, and analyses indicate that the move stems from pressure to lower fuel prices (BBC Business). Putin argued that Russian diesel exports would contribute to the world economy (Anadolu Ajansı). On the oil side, geopolitical risks and declining stocks pushed Brent above 102 dollars, while strong supply keeps prices locked (Ekonomist, CNBC-e).
