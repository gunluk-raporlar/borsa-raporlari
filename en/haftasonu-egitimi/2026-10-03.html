Today's Lesson: Wave Trend oscillator

Hello dear students! Welcome to the Weekend Stock Market School. Today we will learn an indicator that works quietly under the charts but explains momentum very well: the Wave Trend oscillator. Grab your coffee, let's begin!

1. What is it?

Let's start with this analogy: Wave Trend is the EKG of priceEKG It is. The stock's "pulse", i.e. its momentum, is reflected on the screen as two lines.

Net definition: Wave Trend is a momentum oscillator based on price averages, consisting of two lines:

  • WT1: Fast, sensitive line. It detects changes in momentum first.

  • WT2: Smoothed version of WT1. It's calmer and reacts with a delay.

Generally, above + 53 " is considered the "overbought" zone, below - 53 " is the "oversold" zone.

2. Why is it important?

  • Momentum's yön değişimini erken fark etmenize yardım eder.

  • Zirveye yakın coşkuyla, dibe yakın paniği ayırt etme pratiği kazandırır.

  • "It's visually simple: two lines, two thresholds. It's an ideal 'pulse check' for beginners."

  • En önemlisi: Sizi " satın alma dürtüsünü" ölçmeye It trains you. You focus not on where the price is going, but on how strongly it's going.

3. How to Read / Use?

Let's go step by step:

  1. Göstergeyi ekleyin: Select Wave Trend in your charting program (the default settings are suitable for most platforms).
  2. İki çizgiyi izleyin: If WT1 and WT2 are together upward, the momentum is positive; if they are together downward, it's negative.
  3. Kesişimlere bakın: WT1, WT2'yi yukarı keserse momentum yukarı dönmüş demektir. Aşağı keserse momentum zayıflıyor demektir.
  4. Eşikleri okuyun: + Above 53 is overbought, below -53 is oversold. The zero line is the "referee line" for direction: above is bullish, below is bearish.
  5. Look for divergence: If the price makes a new high while the oscillator doesn't, the engine may be overheating but power is decreasing.
  6. Always seek confirmation: A crossover alone is not a signal; read it with price behavior, trend, and support-resistance.

4. Real Example

Let's look at the moving stocks in today's report. Note: The report does not provide the WT value directly; so we'll base our lesson on a "probable reading". It's essential to check the chart for actual interpretation!

  • DSTKF: Last price 694.00 TL, a 9.99% daily drop. Such a sharp drop in momentum significantly pushes downward; Wave Trend lines may have fallen into the negative zone, even below -53. The lesson here: after a sharp drop, don't say "I bought cheap" without checking the oscillator; if the lines are still downward, there is a risk of further decline.

  • SASA: Last price 1.92 TL, a 6.67% daily rise; but the r-value is in a negative zone like -0.41. That is, there is a rise but the "directional strength" is mixed. A WT crossover in this table would be good confirmation. A single-day candle alone doesn't tell the whole story.

  • VAKBN: Last price 33.92 TL, 5.15% daily rise; channel width 41% and r value 0.72 with a strong directional movement chart. Here's the critical lesson: In strong trends, oscillators can stay in the overbought zone for a long time. "+ "I saw 53, I should sell" means missing the trend in the middle of it.

5. Common Mistakes

  • Thinking the threshold alone is a signal: Seeing overbought doesn't mean "sell immediately". A strong trend can keep oscillators at extremes (remember the VAKBN example).

  • Chasing unconfirmed crossovers: In sideways markets WT1/WT2 crossovers often fail; there is a lot of "noise".

  • Looking at only one timeframe: Before panicking when seeing oversold on the daily, check the weekly picture too.

  • Fitting parameters to the past: Playing with settings to find a setting that "catches" every past peak and bottom in history will not work in the future. It brings simplicity.

6. Mini Test

Question 1: If WT1 crosses above WT2 below -53, what could this indicate?

Answer: An upward crossover in the oversold zone indicates that momentum could reverse near the bottom. However, alone it is not enough; price and trend confirmation are sought.

Question 2: Oscillator above +53 and lines are horizontal, while price continues to rise. What should we do?

Answer: " We should not rush to sell just because it is in the "overbought = sell" zone. In a strong trend, the oscillator can stay at extremes; if the upward trend continues while the lines are horizontal, it is more consistent to stay with the trend and wait for an exit signal from WT1 crossing below WT2.

Question 3: The price is making a new peak, but Wave Trend is not exceeding its previous peak. What is this called?

Answer: Negative divergence (divergence). It can be an early warning that the strength of the uptrend is weakening; confirmation is awaited.

7. Summary

  • Wave Trend is a two-line momentum oscillator that measures the pulse of the price: fast WT1, slow WT2.

  • WT1/WT2 crossover signals a momentum change in direction, +53/-53 while its levels show the overbought/oversold zones.

  • In strong trends, the oscillator can stay at extremes for a long time; therefore, it is never a signal on its own.

  • Divergences are an early indicator of momentum loss.

  • Remember: The oscillator shows the way, your risk management is the steering.
