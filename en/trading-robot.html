Strategy Rules
  • BUY: SMA10 is positioned above SMA30 + RSI14 is in the 45–75 band + price is above SMA100 (no fresh crossover expected; entry if pattern exists).
  • SAT: The price crosses below SMA10/SMA30, or falls 3% falls below it (stop), or 3% rises above it (target).
  • Size: at most 12% of equity in a single stock, max 8 positions.
  • Daily brake: if equity falls 3% below yesterday's close, new buys are paused for the day.
  • Costs: commission 0.04% (minimum 10 ₺) + BSMV 15% + legal fee ~0,020% + slippage 0.05%. Total cost for a 12.000 ₺ transaction is ~35–40 ₺ (~0.3%) — the real cost of scalping.
