The 0.67 per cent decline in the XU030 can be explained by the fact that almost all of the index’s weighted shares (THYAO, GARAN, AKBNK, KCHOL, SAHOL) issuing ‘STRONG SELL’ or ‘SELL’ signals; the index’s one-sided downward movement also indicates a weakening of breadth indicators (the number of advancing versus declining shares). The fact that the XU100 fell by a relatively smaller margin than the XU030 (0.65 per cent vs 0.67 per cent) may be a sign that selective buying demand continues in mid- and small-cap shares; however, the fact that only two shares (VAKBN, TRMET) are generating a ‘STRONG BUY’ signal in the technical screening data suggests that market breadth is narrowing.
