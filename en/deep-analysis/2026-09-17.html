Risk Map and Scenarios Under current market conditions, risk management is the most critical factor for protecting portfolio value. Among possible short-term downside scenarios are the Fed adopting a tighter-than-expected policy and increasing global recession concerns. This scenario increases the risk of the BIST 30 index testing the 15.716 support level. On the other hand, rising geopolitical tensions and energy prices permanently exceeding the 71 €/MWh level will create additional pressure on energy and industrial companies. To protect the portfolio against these risks, it is essential to maintain a high cash ratio (40-50%) and make new purchases extremely selective. Leverage should be completely avoided. Stop-loss levels for positions in the portfolio must be set. The upside scenarios are more limited. Scenario 1: A positive surprise regarding the Fed's interest rate decision boosts global risk appetite and the BIST 30 index breaks the 15.716 resistance to rise to the 15.716 level. Scenario 2: An unexpected drop in energy prices leads to improvement in profit margins for petrochemical and energy industrial companies and a recovery in these sectors. However, the likelihood of these scenarios occurring is considered low in light of current macroeconomic data. Therefore, the "survival" principle is at the core of the strategy. The portfolio should be differentiated from technically weak stocks by holding inflation-resistant stocks with solid fundamental financial indicators. As a split strategy, while opening small positions for potential upside opportunities, keeping the majority of the portfolio in safe assets would be the most logical approach. 6.
