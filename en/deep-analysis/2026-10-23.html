In technical scan data, "channel %" values show how close prices are to the upper or lower boundary of the regression channel. Positive channel % values (example: SAHOL 47%, KCHOL 4%) indicate the price is at the upper part of the channel, negative values (example: GUBRF %‑15, EREGL %‑36) point to the lower part.

  • Positive channel: Stocks such as SAHOL, KCHOL, GARAN, YKBNK have brought their prices close to the upper boundary of the channel; this indicates that short-term resistance levels are close and the rally may be limited.

  • Negative channel: Stocks such as GUBRF, EREGL, TRALT, SASA, on the other hand, are moving in the lower part of the channel; this situation points to the oversold region, while at the same time carrying the risk that the price may fall further.

3.2 Wave Trend (WT) and Pearson r

WT signals are either "BUY" or "SELL", and the Pearson r correlation coefficient measures the strength of the price movement trend. An r value close to +1 indicates a positive trend, while a value close to –1 indicates a negative trend.

  • Positive r (> 0.4): Banking stocks such as VAKBN (+0.50), ISCTR (+0.28), YKBNK (+0.28) show that prices are in a slight upward tendency; however, since WT is "SELL", short-term pressure still persists.
