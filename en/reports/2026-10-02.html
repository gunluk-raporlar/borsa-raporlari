Turkey's macro data prove that the inflation-interest rate spiral is gradually breaking, but the real impact via the credit channel has not yet fully materialized. The contradiction between the technical rally and macro fundamentals depends on three critical conditions for sustainability: 1) Today's US NFP data staying within the 100-150 thousand range keeps Fed rate cut expectations alive, 2) USD/TR peacefully remaining within the 49.00-49.50 band and reserve flows turning positive, 3) The slowdown in bank loans (annual 36% level) falling below the excessive threshold allowing the real sector to breathe.
