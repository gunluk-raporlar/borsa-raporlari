The index's -0.12% performance in dollar terms shows that it remains within a horizontal range at current levels, but upward sustainability requires currency stability. If USD/TRY continues to rise, dollar-based losses may increase despite horizontal TRY-based closing prices, potentially increasing selling pressure, especially in banking and aviation stocks with high foreign participation. Therefore, the index's holding at 15.946.95 should not be evaluated as a technical recovery signal without currency movement stabilization; the current outlook points to a scenario where selective stock movements are prominent but direction uncertainty continues for the overall index.
