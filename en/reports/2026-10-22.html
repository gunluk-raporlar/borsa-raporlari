3. Macro Outlook and News Flow

At the close last evening, USD/TRY was almost flat at 49,5974 (daily change +0.06%), while the dollar-based return of the BIST 30 index at -1.52% came in below the TL-based -1.45%; this sharp divergence signals an acceleration of foreign portfolio outflows and that domestic institutional buying power cannot offset exchange rate risk. This picture supports the preservation of net interest margins in the banking sector (Yapı Kredi, Garanti BBVA), while in the real sector imported input cost pressure continues to squeeze margins; if monthly inflation in the October TÜFE data comes in above2.5%, real interest rate expectations will turn again and the relative resilience in bank stocks could turn into selling pressure.

Geopolitical tension in the Middle East (alarm over attacks on Saudi Arabian oil facilities, withdrawal of US personnel) is pushing Brent crude to the 109.99 USD/barrel level, while WTI's3.67% decline shows that the supply risk premium is regionally focused. While Türkiye's August current account deficit is measured at 4.05 billion USD, the rise in imports to 31.21 billion USD (exports 25.98 billion USD) reminds of the current account's sensitivity to energy prices; if oil stays above 110 USD, the current account deficit targets will be put at risk and expectations of TL depreciation will be triggered. The rising trend in US 10-year Treasury yields (5-year4.97%) is strengthening the "risk-off" tone against emerging market assets; the low level of VIX at 17.16 shows that market players have not yet entered panic selling, while the withdrawal of liquidity is proceeding slowly. In the Euro Zone, the weakening of August industrial production at -0.5% monthly and -1.1% annually, together with core inflation staying at2.5%, makes it difficult for the ECB to take steps on the easing path; this also ties the sustainability of the 55.68 level in the EUR/TRY parity to corporate FX selling. In this framework, macro data are moving in line with technical signals (average RSI 38, "STRONG SELL" in 20 of the 30 stocks); however, if USD/TRY breaks the 49.70 resistance and accelerates towards 50.00, technical oversold levels will become invalid and the index could deepen towards the 14.200-14.000 band.
