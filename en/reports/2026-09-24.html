This macro dataset reveals that the rise in the BIST 30 is driven by an easing of global risk appetite and the demand strength of local sectors despite local macro pressures, but sustainability in a high-interest environment still depends on risk appetite. The performance of these stocks reflects the volatility of global oil prices and Turkey's energy needs, as well as the pressure on industrial production and commercial demand. This mechanism triggers divergence across sectors while also bringing the risk of increasing the weight of energy stocks in portfolios. The recovery in the BIST 30 also parallels the performance of the banking sector. In a high-interest environment, while the collection power in banks' loan portfolios increases, deposit costs also rise; however, if demand persists, banks' interest income may remain above costs and preserve profitability.
