Executive Summary and Market Overview Markets are following an extremely volatile and selective path under the shadow of global and macroeconomic uncertainties. The BIST 30 index continues its consolidation at the 16,645 resistance level. A permanent recovery signal has not yet formed below this critical level, and the index's attempt to hold above this band indicates the current market uncertainty. The key risks highlighted in previous analyses; the liquidity effect that the US Federal Reserve's (Fed) interest rate decision in September will create, the reflection of geopolitical tensions originating from Iran on energy prices, and Turkey's high inflation (28.4%) expectation, remain at the center of current market dynamics. In this environment, the fundamental perspective of fund management focuses on creating controlled positions on selected stocks with strong technical and fundamental resilience, with high liquidity protection rather than aggressive buying strategies. Markets are closely monitoring the Fed's decision and geopolitical developments, aiming to maintain flexibility by increasing the cash portion of the portfolio if necessary. 2. News and Macroeconomic Assessment The main flows determining the agenda emerge as macroeconomic and geopolitical factors that directly affect the performance of BIST 30 companies. At the global level, the Fed's September meeting and US inflation data are the key factors determining global risk appetite. Goldman Sachs' assessment that financial conditions have eased in Turkey provides a partial easing signal regarding the local liquidity environment, but high inflation expectations (28.4%) continue to pressure consumer spending and companies' cost structures. This situation is limiting growth potential, especially in retail and service sectors dependent on domestic demand. The geopolitical front is extremely sensitive; tensions in relations with Iran are putting pressure on energy and logistics costs, negatively impacting energy-intensive sectors (Ereğli Demir Çelik, Tüpraş, Tosya). Natural gas prices remaining above the 71 €/MWh level pose risks to industrial production and the current account deficit. On the other hand, structural developments such as the additional $1.1 billion provided to Halkbank provide short-term support to the banking sector, but the long-term impact remains limited. This contradictory macro environment keeps investor psychology anxious and causes the market to seek "safe havens". 3rd Technical Evaluation (Stock-based) The technical anatomy of leading stocks reflects the uncertainty in the overall market and shows how critical selection is. Company and Financial Assessment Based on fundamental data, the financial profiles of BIST 30 companies are being tested for resilience against macroeconomic challenges. The size of current assets is an important indicator in terms of a company's operational flexibility and market dominance. In this context, holding companies such as KCHOL (Koç Holding) and SAHOL (Sabancı Holding) maintain their leadership in their sectors with massive current assets of 16,645 billion TL and 2,790 billion TL, respectively. These structures appear more resilient to a single sectoral shock thanks to their diversified business lines. However, companies operating in commodity-intensive sectors such as energy and metals are experiencing margin pressure due to high natural gas and energy costs. For example, companies like EREGL (Ereğli Demir Çelik) and SISE (Şişecam) are struggling to pass on cost increases to prices. The banking sector, in a high inflation and interest rate environment, is trying to balance between credit growth and net interest margin. In this environment, banks with high capital adequacy ratios and strong liquidity are standing out. However, the "STRONG SELL" signals in technical analysis indicate that a cautious approach to the banking sector is generally required. In addition to financial ratios, companies' debt structures and foreign currency borrowing levels create important risk factors against exchange rate fluctuations. 5. Risk Management and Strategy Under current market conditions, risk management is the most critical factor determining portfolio performance. Short-term scenarios require creating a protective shield by analyzing potential downside and upside risks.Downside Risk Scenario: If the US Fed makes a more hawkish announcement than expected (indicating interest rate stability or future rate hikes instead of a new cut expectation) or if Iran-Middle East tensions escalate, energy prices and global risk appetite could be negatively affected immediately. In this case, it is highly likely that the BIST 30 index will test the 16,645 support level and a sharp sell-off will occur in risky sectors such as banking and aviation. In this scenario, the cash ratio in the portfolio should be increased to the 40-50% level and strict discipline should be applied at stop-loss levels (for example, KCHOL 210 TL, BIMAS 415 TL).Upward Risk Scenario: If the Fed gives a clear signal of interest rate cuts and geopolitical tensions follow a peaceful course, the BIST 30 index could break the 16,645 resistance and create an upward potential towards the 16,645 band. In this scenario, the high cash ratio will present an opportunity to increase positions in strategically held stocks.Strategic Approach: Both scenarios indicate that in the current uncertainty environment, aggressive leverage should be avoided, position sizes should be reduced, and flexibility against market volatility should be maintained with high liquidity. Hedging is about knowing how to take positions in the right stocks with the right weights, rather than selling the entire portfolio. 6.
