Executive Summary and Market Overview The BIST 30 index continues to consolidate between the 16.182 level, which has been a significant resistance band in recent weeks. This range stands out as a critical test area for determining the market direction. The index breaking above this area is considered a potential trend reversal signal; if it fails to break, the likelihood of testing support at the 16.182 level increases. Market psychology is following a cautious path due to global central bank policies, geopolitical risks, and domestic inflationary pressures. In this environment, fund management prioritizes capital preservation and creating selective buying opportunities. While the performance in energy and retail stocks, which were prominent in previous periods, is subject to sustainability tests, the weakness in the banking and holding sectors necessitates careful selection in portfolio composition. Among the day's most critical developments are trade tensions between Germany and China, increased volatility in global bond markets, and US inflation data. These factors have the potential to instantly risk appetite and reinforce the necessity of maintaining a flexible cash position in portfolio management. 2. News and Macroeconomic Assessment The macroeconomic ground presents both opportunities and serious risks for BIST 30 companies. The annual inflation rate rising to 31.5% continues to pressure consumer spending and companies' cost structures. This situation is eroding profit margins, especially in energy and input-sensitive industrial and construction sectors, while providing relative protection to retail and consumer staples sectors with inflation-resistant business models. Although the Energy Minister's messages on supply security contribute positively, natural gas prices remaining above the 71 €/MWh level pose a significant risk factor for energy-intensive industries. The global front, on the other hand, presents a complex picture. Germany's preparation of economic measures against China and the AI tension between the US and China are creating uncertainty in global trade flows. While Goldman Sachs' assessment that financial conditions have eased in Turkey indicates some increase in liquidity, this easing has limited impact on the real sector in a high inflation environment. Particularly developments such as cuts in social assistance and minimum wage increases are pressuring consumer confidence and negatively affecting domestic demand dynamics. In this environment, investor psychology has shifted to defense mode, and stocks with dividend potential and inflation protection are coming to the forefront. Investors need to act with a highly sensitive approach to changes in global interest rate policies and geopolitical developments. 3rd Technical Evaluation (Stock-based) The BIST 30 index's ability to hold above the 16.182 resistance is the most critical issue from a technical analysis perspective. Risk Management and Strategy Current market conditions are placing risk management at the center of portfolio management. Investors should adopt strict strategies to manage both potential downside losses and upside opportunities in a balanced manner. Short-term Scenarios: Downside Scenario (Risky): The biggest risk is the possibility of the BIST 30 index breaking the 16.182 resistance and pulling back toward the 16.182 support level. In this scenario, a sharp sell-off could occur, especially in energy and holding stocks. In this case, risky stocks in the portfolio should be sold quickly and the cash position should be increased. Strictly applying stop-loss levels (such as 405-410 for TUPRS, 415 TL for BIMAS) is critically important for capital preservation in this scenario. Upside Scenario (Opportunity): The index breaking the 16.182 resistance with high volume and permanently will be a signal for the continuation of the uptrend. In this case, potential gains could emerge in fundamentally and technically strong stocks such as TUPRS and BIMAS. In this scenario, the portfolio's performance can be improved by gradually using the cash position in these stocks. Portfolio Protection Shields: 1. High Cash Ratio: Keeping at least 35-40% of the portfolio in cash is the most basic protection shield against sudden market fluctuations. This cash provides flexibility to take advantage of a possible pullback or to meet unexpected cash needs. 2. Stop-Loss Discipline: Strict adherence to pre-determined stop-loss levels for each stock is required. Orders should be placed in advance and not adjusted according to market conditions. This discipline prevents small losses from turning into big ones. 3. Sectoral Diversification: Over-concentration in a single sector should be avoided. In our current portfolio recommendation, a distribution is expected with a focus on energy and retail sectors. This helps preserve the overall performance of the portfolio in the event of a sectoral shock. 4. Avoid Leverage Usage: In a high volatility and uncertain environment, using leverage can lead to rapid erosion of capital. The portfolio should be managed entirely with its own capital. Strategic Approach: Our strategy is to adopt a cautious and selective stance. The "Up Step Rule" is not a standalone buy signal. Only controlled purchases should be made in stocks with strong technical and fundamental data. Regardless of market conditions, a portion of capital should always be kept liquid. This approach protects capital during possible pullbacks while allowing to benefit from opportunities at the start of an uptrend. 6.
