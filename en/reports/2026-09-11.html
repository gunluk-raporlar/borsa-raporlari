It is essential to create portfolio protection shields against these downside risks. The most basic shield is a high cash and short-term bond ratio. At least 40% of the portfolio should be held in liquid assets to keep the necessary capital ready for creating buying opportunities when the market falls. This prevents the loss from growing and protects capital. Finally, leverage should be completely avoided.
