3. Macro Outlook and News Flow

While USD/TRY follows an almost unchanged course at 49.57 (daily +0.09%), the BIST 30 index rising 3.20% in TL terms and 3.15% in dollar terms indicates that daily purchasing power is not fueled by exchange rate volatility but entirely by domestic demand dynamics. According to Turkish statistics, September CPI monthly 1.84% (below estimate 2.3%) and annual 29.73% (previous 31.51%) continues the disinflation trend; annual PPI also declined to 27.38% (from 27.95%), strengthening positive real interest rates. These data expand the TCMB's room to maintain its 37% policy rate while positively revising net interest margin expectations for the banking sector (Yapı Kredi, Garanti BBVA); softening in deposit costs (weighted deposit rate fell to 43.12%) and controlled slowdown in loan growth (36.32% annual) support margin quality.

The divergence in oil prices (Brent down 0.5% while WTI fell sharply 5.45%) and the BIST's resilience to the oil shock despite the Middle East agenda reflect external balance confidence supported by the current account turning to a surplus of 4.05 billion dollars in August data and central reserves rising to 64.57 billion dollars. The current account surplus of 4 billion dollars after June allows the currency risk premium to compress and foreign flows toward TL assets to continue.

Looking through the monetary policy transmission channel; positive real interest rates (policy rate 37% vs CPI 29.73%) shift the savings tendency from housing and foreign currency toward the real sector and equities; retail sales growing 1.4% monthly and 10.1% annually in August supports this evidence. However, industrial production contracting 1.0% monthly and 1.86% annually in August, and the September budget deficit jumping to 532.2 billion TL (from a 12.9 billion surplus the previous month), raise questions about the quality of domestic demand strength and fiscal discipline. The consumer confidence index remaining at 91.9 (below 91.9) indicates that the erosion in household purchasing power has not yet fully recovered.

Invalidation conditions: If USD/TRY breaks the 49,569 psychological resistance on volume and makes it permanent (the real effective exchange rate index at 105.45 is already above its historical averages), if the disinflation trend breaks with the September data (if October TÜFE comes in above2.5% monthly) or if the US 10-year Treasury yield stays above3.7%, this bank-heavy rally could come under selling pressure.
