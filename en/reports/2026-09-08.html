1. Executive Summary and Market Overview

The BIST 30 index, which has been following a strong cautious and selective trend, experienced a short-term breather while continuing its "Up Step Rule" effect throughout the past week, but could not completely eliminate the fundamental pressure created by macroeconomic uncertainties. The index is still moving within the boundaries of the oversold zone due to geopolitical risks (tensions originating from Iran) and the global energy shock (natural gas prices staying above 71 euros).

As emphasized in our previous analyses, the rebound that occurred on Friday is not a signal of a permanent trend reversal; it is evaluated as a "breathing" process where volatility increased and investors tried to evaluate their cash positions. In the current market dynamics, investor psychology oscillates between "fear of loss" and "opportunity seeking". From a fund management perspective, the index staying below the 4.200 level and the underperformance of energy-intensive sectors (EREGL, TUPRS) are pushing the overall market towards a "safe haven" search.

Fundamental Perspective:The market needs a clear macro signal (ECB interest rate decision and US inflation data) and for the index to break above key resistance levels (4.200-4.250). Until this stage, the strategy of "cash protection" and "focusing on core components (FMCG, Content, Safe Banks)" in the portfolio structure is the safest shield.

2. News and Macroeconomic Assessment

Geopolitical and Energy Agenda:
Escalating tensions originating from Iran and "economic war" rhetoric continue to negatively affect global risk appetite. This situation is particularly pressuring energy-intensive production and transportation sectors (EREGL, TUPRS, TOASO). However, the increase in Iraqi oil exports and the approach of EU exports to the $72 billion level positively affect the external balance, potentially easing the negative sentiment on the stock market.

Macroeconomic Spiral and Inflation:
Domestic macro data continues to worry investors. The inflation expectation remaining at the 28.4% level causes real returns to erode and leads investors to prefer cash assets. US inflation data and the ECB interest rate decision will be a critical test point next week. If global central banks raise interest rates, it could create a foreign source-based selling pressure on BIST.

News Flow and Investor Psychology:
The $1.1 billion source provided to Halkbank provides short-term support to the banking sector, but whether this support is a permanent reversal signal is debatable. Executive departures at OpenAI and fluctuations in global technology companies are limiting risk appetite. Investors are turning to safe havens like "cultural products" (BIMAS, KCHOL) and "gold" that offer high real returns.
