🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Petrochemical margins are determined by global naphtha-product spread; excess supply from Asia is pressuring margins, while capacity closures in Europe will be a balancing factor in the medium term. Domestically, USD/TL at 48.83 provides natural protection by partially balancing import raw material costs with foreign exchange revenues; however, real appreciation of TL poses a risk to export margins. The decline in inflation to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations are the most important macro catalysts that could alleviate the company's high financing costs. BofA's acquisition of Socar Turkey shares confirms continued foreign institutional interest in Turkish petrochemical assets; similar strategic moves could raise the valuation floor in the sector. The decline in oil prices reduces naphtha costs, working in favor of the spread. Main risks; extension of global supply surplus, energy cost shocks, and real appreciation of TL.

🧾 Balance Sheet Evaluation

Petkim increased its sales by 28.0% in the first half of 2026 but fell into loss territory with a 199.9% decline in net profit; however, in the second quarter, it gave a strong signal of profitability turnaround with 38.3 billion TL in sales (+37.3%) and 4.1 billion TL in net profit. Over the last four quarters, there are 119.2 billion TL in sales against 3.3 billion TL in net loss; therefore, the P/E ratio is not meaningful at this stage. The company is trading below its equity with a market cap of ~58 billion TL (P/B ~0.8x); this discount reflects the perception of being at the bottom of the petrochemical cycle and the squeeze in the naphtha-product spread. The return to profit in 2Q had a high contribution from financial income and currency effects; for permanent recovery of operating margin, the spread between naphtha costs and product prices needs to widen. Financial debt/equity is high at 74.5%; ~49 billion TL in net debt limits balance sheet flexibility during cyclical downturns. ROE is -4.3%; several strong quarters are needed to cover TTM losses. BofA's share acquisition is important as it provides a corporate reference to asset value. In summary, Petkim presents a high risk-high return profile with cyclical bottom, deep asset discount, and strategic partner interest; the fundamental story depends on spread recovery.
