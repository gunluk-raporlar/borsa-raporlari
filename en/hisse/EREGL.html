The steel sector is priced at the intersection of the global commodity cycle and domestic demand: China's supply discipline and Europe's protectionist measures support prices, while the construction and automotive demand trajectory in Turkey determines volumes. The decline in domestic inflation to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations have the potential to boost credit-financed demand in steel-intensive sectors like automotive and white goods. USD/TL's position at 48.83 provides natural protection by partially balancing foreign exchange-based raw material costs with export revenues; energy cost trends continue to be the main determinant of margins. The metal main industry index's rise of over 2.8% indicates that cyclical recovery expectations are being priced in. Although the carbon border tax (CBAM) compliance process increases export costs, Erdemir's scale and efficiency maintain its competitive edge. Main risks; reversal in global steel prices and shocks in energy costs.
