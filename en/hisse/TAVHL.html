🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Airport operations is a foreign exchange-weighted model fed by tourism revenues; strong foreign tourist arrivals to Turkey and the extended season support TAV's traffic revenues. A significant portion of revenues is euro-based, so the trend of EUR/TL and USD/TL directly affects profitability; USD/TL trading at 48.83 supports this channel. Inflation falling to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations are the most important macro catalysts to reduce the company's high project financing burden. Global aviation demand remaining above pre-pandemic levels provides a structural tailwind. The resilience of travel demand even with gram gold at 6.786 TL confirms the shift in consumer behavior priorities. Main risks; geopolitical developments' impact on tourism flows, euro weakness, and uncertainties regarding concession periods.

🧾 Balance Sheet Evaluation

TAV increased its sales by 32.9% in the first half of 2026; in the second quarter, it recorded 26.7 billion TL in sales (+36.9%) and 3.8 billion TL in net profit, marking a strong rebound from last year's weak base. Quarterly net margin of 14.1% is at the opposite end of last year's first half's -5.0%; increased passenger traffic and strengthened commercial area revenues drive profitability. The company, which generated 107.9 billion TL in sales and 6.7 billion TL in net profit over the last four quarters, has a market cap of ~102 billion TL; with P/E ~15.2x, P/B ~1.2x and EV/EBITDA ~7.4x, its valuation is reasonable compared to global airport operators, below its historical averages. Although financial debt/equity of 134.3% appears high, most of it is long-term project financing for concession investments; net debt ~94.6 billion TL is ~3.6x EBITDA and within sector norms. ROE of 8.1% is in a recovery trend; strong second-half seasonal profits could push it higher. 1.3% dividend yield is limited. In summary, TAV is a quality infrastructure asset with reasonable multiples, foreign exchange-based revenue structure, and strong seasonal dynamics; the main valuation support is the sustainability of passenger growth.
