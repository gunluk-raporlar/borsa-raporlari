🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Airport operations is a foreign exchange-weighted model fed by tourism revenues; strong foreign tourist arrivals to Turkey and the extended season support TAV's traffic revenues. A significant portion of revenues is euro-based, so the trend of EUR/TL and USD/TL directly affects profitability; USD/TL trading at 48.83 supports this channel. Inflation falling to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations are the most important macro catalysts to reduce the company's high project financing burden. Global aviation demand remaining above pre-pandemic levels provides a structural tailwind. The resilience of travel demand even with gram gold at 6.786 TL confirms the shift in consumer behavior priorities. Main risks; geopolitical developments' impact on tourism flows, euro weakness, and uncertainties regarding concession periods.

TAV increased its sales by 32.9% in the first half of 2026; in the second quarter, it recorded 26.7 billion TL in sales (+36.9%) and 3.8 billion TL in net profit, marking a strong rebound from last year's weak base.
