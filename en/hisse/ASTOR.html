🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

The decline in domestic inflation to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations could stimulate domestic market orders by facilitating the financing of investment expenditures. As the macro easing cycle deepens, there may be an increased orientation towards high-multiple growth stocks; Astor's net cash position provides an additional advantage in this scenario.

The company, which generated 42.9 billion TL in sales and 11.2 billion TL in net profit over the last four quarters, has a market value of ~265 billion TL; accordingly, P/E ~23.7x, P/B ~6.1x, EV/EBITDA ~20.4x. These multiples are above the BIST industrial average; the market is giving a significant premium for Astor's energy transformation-themed growth story and export potential. The balance sheet is strong: financial debt/equity is only 13.6 and the company has a net cash position of ~9.4 billion TL; this limits financing cost pressure in an interest rate cut environment. ROE is 25.6%, close to inflation and above the sector average. Key monitoring points; sustainability of the margin jump in 2Q, capacity utilization rates, and the trajectory of export orders. In summary, Astor's operational story is strong and its balance sheet is clean; however, current valuation multiples are pricing in a significant portion of the growth, making the stock sensitive to news flow.
