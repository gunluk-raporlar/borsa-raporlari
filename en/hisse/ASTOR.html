As the macro easing cycle deepens, there may be an increased orientation towards high-multiple growth stocks; Astor's net cash position provides an additional advantage in this scenario.

In summary, Astor's operational story is strong and its balance sheet is clean; however, current valuation multiples are pricing in a significant portion of the growth, making the stock sensitive to news flow.
