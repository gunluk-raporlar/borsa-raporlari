🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Telecom sector is experiencing the classic dilemma of disinflation period: while cost inflation eases, delayed and regulated tariff structure limits revenue side. Inflation falling to 31.5% opens room for real recovery in second half tariff adjustments; CBRT's easing path eases financing costs. USD/TL's stable position at 48.83 balances device and infrastructure import costs; increasing network's local equipment share reduces currency risk. Structural growth in data center and cloud demand creates new revenue pool for Turkcell's TechFin and digital services arms. Amid uncertainty with gram gold at 6.786 TL, interest in high-yield defensive stocks is strengthening; Turkcell fits this profile with ~4% yield. Main risks; tightening in tariff regulation, price pressure from competition and spectrum investment costs.

Turkcell increased sales by 5.7% and net profit by 3.3% in the first half of 2026; in Q2, it drew a shrinking profitability picture with 72.5 billion TL sales (+2.7%) and 5.6 billion TL net profit (-4.7%). Quarterly net margin at 7.7% parallels 7.6% in first half of last year; tariff increases lagging behind inflation pressurize real profitability. The company, which generated 277.1 billion TL sales and 21.1 billion TL net profit in last four quarters, has market cap ~219 billion TL; with P/E ~10.4x, P/B ~0.7x and EV/EBITDA ~5.8x, trading below global telecom thresholds. Main reason for this discount is pricing power limited by regulation and competition; in return, defensive cash flow and strong subscriber base form valuation base. Financial debt/equity 68.5%, net debt ~96.9 billion TL at ~1.8x EBITDA; leverage is manageable and significant portion of debt is long-term. ROE 6.8% significantly below inflation; Data center and digital services investments offer medium-term growth option. 4.0% dividend yield is a strong support factor. In summary, Turkcell is a defensive, dividend-paying and discounted telecom asset; catalysts being tariff adjustments approaching inflation rate and acceleration of digital services revenues.
