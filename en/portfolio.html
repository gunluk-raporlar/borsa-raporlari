Demo Portfolio

A virtual portfolio of 100.000 TL equally distributed among BIST 30 stocks. No buying or selling is done, only tracked; automatically updated every weekday morning with the previous trading day's closing prices.

📐 Methodology — how is this portfolio calculated?

• On 2026-09-06, 100.000 TL invested in BIST 30 stocks evenly distributed; quantities are fractional; in practice, rounding to whole lots is required.

• Every weekday morning compared with the previous trading day's closing prices updated; on holidays, the value does not change, no buying or selling is done.

• Dividends, bonus shares and stock splits are currently not accounted for; there may be return deviations on the days these events occur.

• Comparison lines (gold, dollar, deposit, BIST 100, BIST 30, inflation) are normalized to the same 100.000 TL base; the inflation value is the latest TÜIK annual reading from the TradingView economic calendar and is reflected on the chart with the period date.

• Total return is not the average of individual stock percentages, but the total value change of the portfolio. The net return scenario on the Signal Report page uses a ~0.10% commission+BSMV assumption.

This is a virtual test portfolio; not investment advice. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.
