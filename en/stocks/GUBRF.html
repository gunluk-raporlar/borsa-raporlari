🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

The profitability of the fertilizer sector is sensitive to the spread between natural gas costs and global fertilizer prices; while stable natural gas prices support the cost side, normalization in product prices narrows margins. The strategic importance of agricultural production and food security priorities provide a structural demand base for the sector; government support and subsidy policies are parameters to monitor. The decline in inflation to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations have limited financial impact due to the company's low leverage structure; the main channel is farmer purchasing power through agricultural credit conditions. USD/TL trading at 48.83 level partially balances import raw material costs with export revenues. With gold at 6.786 TL and risk appetite selective, defensive balance sheet industrial stocks are relatively sheltered. Main risks; decline in global urea/ammonia prices and shock in energy costs.
