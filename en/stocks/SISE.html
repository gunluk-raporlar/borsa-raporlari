🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Demand in the glass sector is at the intersection of construction, automotive, and packaging cycles: expectations of interest rate cuts domestically have the potential to boost construction activity, while weak industrial production in Europe is pressuring export volumes. The decline in inflation to 31.5% improves cost predictability; stability in natural gas prices supports energy-intensive production costs. The USD/TL rate at 48.83 provides a currency advantage to the company, with more than half of its revenues being international; the real appreciation of the TL is the main risk eroding this advantage. The formation of a bottom in global glass prices and limited Chinese exports could support margins. High net debt transforms the Central Bank of the Republic of Turkey's (TCMB) rate cut path into a critical variable for the company. Main risks; prolonged weakness in European demand, energy shock, and high investment financing costs.
