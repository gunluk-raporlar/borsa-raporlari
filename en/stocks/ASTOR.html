🌍 Macroeconomic Evaluation

Energy transformation and grid investments structurally support transformer demand on a global scale; Astor is one of the leading representatives of this theme on BIST. The decline in domestic inflation to 31.5% and interest rate cut expectations could stimulate domestic market orders by facilitating the financing of investment expenditures. With USD/TL at 48.83, export revenues provide a currency advantage; modernization programs in European and Middle Eastern grids keep external demand alive. In contrast, price volatility in basic inputs such as copper and electrical steel creates risks on margins; the real appreciation trend of the TL could also limit export competitiveness. As the macro easing cycle deepens, there may be an increased orientation towards high-multiple growth stocks; Astor's net cash position provides an additional advantage in this scenario.

🧾 Balance Sheet Evaluation

In the first half of 2026, Astor increased its sales by 7.5% and raised its net profit by 87.7%; in the second quarter, it recorded a margin jump with 9.9 billion TL in sales and 2.8 billion TL in net profit (+120% annually). The quarterly net margin reached 28.5%, double the 13.8% in the first half of the previous year; improvement in product composition and normalization in financing costs support profitability. The company, which generated 42.9 billion TL in sales and 11.2 billion TL in net profit over the last four quarters, has a market value of ~265 billion TL; accordingly, P/E ~23.7x, P/B ~6.1x, EV/EBITDA ~20.4x. These multiples are above the BIST industrial average; the market is giving a significant premium for Astor's energy transformation-themed growth story and export potential. The balance sheet is strong: financial debt/equity is only 13.6 and the company has a net cash position of ~9.4 billion TL; this limits financing cost pressure in an interest rate cut environment. ROE is 25.6%, close to inflation and above the sector average. Key monitoring points; sustainability of the margin jump in 2Q, capacity utilization rates, and the trajectory of export orders. In summary, Astor's operational story is strong and its balance sheet is clean; however, current valuation multiples are pricing in a significant portion of the growth, making the stock sensitive to news flow.
