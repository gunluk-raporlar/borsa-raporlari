The USD/TL's trajectory at the 48.83 level supports foreign project revenues, while the real appreciation of the TL partially erodes the cost advantage. In general assessment, Enka appeals to the value investor profile with its clean balance sheet, high dividend, and reasonable multiples; the main risks are the cyclicality of construction margins and the volatility of subsidiary profits.
