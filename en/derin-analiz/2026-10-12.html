Portföy performansı 6 Eylül 2026 başlangıcından bu yana %-6.77 kayıpla XU100 (-13.32%) ve XU030 (-9.47%) referans endekslerine göre nispeten daha az kayıplı seyretse de, nakit ağırlıklı pozisyonlama (portföy 93,234 TL) piyasa neutralitesini yansıtmaktadır.

Index Structure and Breadth

The 0.67 per cent decline in the XU030 can be explained by the fact that almost all of the index’s weighted shares (THYAO, GARAN, AKBNK, KCHOL, SAHOL) issuing ‘STRONG SELL’ or ‘SELL’ signals; the index’s one-sided downward movement also indicates a weakening of breadth indicators (the number of advancing versus declining shares). The fact that the XU100 fell by a relatively smaller margin than the XU030 (0.65 per cent vs 0.67 per cent) may be a sign that selective buying demand continues in mid- and small-cap shares; however, the fact that only two shares (VAKBN, TRMET) are generating a ‘STRONG BUY’ signal in the technical screening data suggests that market breadth is narrowing.

Effects of Foreign Exchange and Commodities

The fact that the USD/TRY exchange rate has remained stable at 49.33 indicates that, within the framework of the CBRT’s management of its foreign exchange reserves (60.33 billion USD, as at 8 October) and the real effective exchange rate index (105.45, September data), indicating that the exchange rate is being maintained within a controlled band. The rise in the price of gold to 4.216 USD per ounce (+1.43 per cent) and the increase in the price of gold per gram to 6.653 TL can be explained by a combination of the weakening US Dollar Index (DXY 102.21) and falling oil prices (Brent 104.72, WTI 91.85); this situation presents a positive outlook for gold mining and precious metals portfolios, whilst painting a mixed picture for the margins of Tüpraş (TUPRS) and the petrochemical sector (PETKM, ENKAI).
