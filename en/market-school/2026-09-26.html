Today we will learn an indicator that answers the question "is there a trend in the market or not?": ADX. Grab your coffee, let's get started. 1. What is it? First, a simple analogy: When you're driving a car, you look at the speedometer. The speedometer tells you how fast how fast ; but it's going it does not tell. You can drive fast forward or backward. ADX for the market is exactly this: It measures how much the price is moving in a stable and strong manner. It doesn't tell the direction. Clear definition: ADX (Average Directional Index), "J. Welts Wilder developed indicator that shows the strength of a trend with a value between "0 and 100 . It is generally calculated with a 14 period. The nearby+DI and-DI lines also help with direction. 2. Why is it important? It reduces false signals: In sideways markets, trend indicators can be misleading. ADX answers the question "Is there really a trend here?". It helps you pick the right tool: In a strong trend, a trend-following approach is suitable; in weak ADX, support-resistance logic is more appropriate. It builds patience: It teaches you to "wait for confirmation" while ADX is below 25. It also sees declines: A strong downward movement also raises ADX. This is valuable information for risk management. 3. How to Read / Use? Let’s go step by step: Open the indicator: Add the ADX to the chart (default period 14). Look at the level: 0 – 25: Weak zone; no trend or very weak (sideways market). 25 – 50: Strong trend. The most commonly used threshold in practice: Above 25: strong trend. 50 – 75: Very strong trend. Above 75: Extremely strong; the trend may be mature, even close to weakening. 3. Watch the slope: Rising ADX means the trend is strengthening; falling ADX means it is weakening. 4. For direction, look at the DI lines: ADX does not tell direction. When +DI is on top, buying pressure dominates; when -DI is on top, selling pressure dominates. 5. Do not use it alone: Confirm with volume and price action. Keep this in mind: " ADX measures strength, not direction." 4. Real Example Let's look at a few names from our site's daily data: KRDMD: It is on the strong buy signal list. Last price 46.96 TL, daily 1.65% gain. Channel width 54%, r value 0.77. A high r means the move is consistent in a certain direction. The critical educational question: is ADX also above 25? If the answer is "yes", the strength aspect of the signal is confirmed too. If ADX is below 20, be cautious despite the label. DSTKF: Last price 1,174.00 TL, daily 9.97% loss; channel 26%, r -0.50. . The lesson here: a sharp drop can raise ADX over time; but ADX does not say it is downward , only that strength is increasing . Direction is shown by the price and the DI lines. PETKM: Last price 21.06 TL, daily 4.27% drop; but r 0.49, that is positive. Isn't it interesting? One day being red does not break the period's general consistency. ADX is the same way: It looks at the average of the period not one day. GUBRF: Last price 458.00 TL, daily 6.45% increase; channel 35%, r 0.51. A moderately positive consistency profile. Did you see? r and ADX are like brothers: Both look at the period, not one day. 5. Common Mistakes " To say "ADX rose, buy signal!" ADX doesn't indicate direction; it indicates strength. For direction, look at +DI/-DI and price movement. Mistaking the 25 threshold as a trading signal by itself. ADX above 25 means "the trend is strong"; it does not mean "buy" or "sell". Drawing quick conclusions from a single-day spike. ADX is smoothed and lagging; a single bad day won't immediately drop it. Forcing a trend strategy in low ADX. Following trends in a sideways market generates constantly false signals. 6. Mini Test Question 1: ADX is at 18 and moving sideways. What should you think about in this market? Answer: Trend is weak or non-existent; market is sideways. Trend-following signals may not be reliable; support-resistance approach may be more appropriate. Question 2: ADX is 42 and rising; +DI line is above -DI. What does it mean? Answer: There is a strong uptrend. ADX strength, position of DI lines indicates direction. Question 3: A stock fell 9.97% in a single day. Would ADX spike immediately and say "downtrend has begun"? Answer: No. ADX is lagging; one day won't make it spike immediately. Even if it rises over time, it doesn't indicate direction; price and DI lines indicate the decline. 7. Summary ADX, the trend'snot the direction but the strength measures; gives a value between 0–100. above 25 is considered a strong trend, below is considered horizontal/weak market. For direction, look at the + DI / -DI lines and price. ADX is lagging; looks at the period, not single-day movements. In a strong trend, trend following; with weak ADX, support-resistance logic is more suitable. This lesson is for educational purposes, not investment advice.
