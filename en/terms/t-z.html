Technical Analysis Technical Analysis

The making of price forecasts for the future by analyzing past-period market movements on the basis of indicators such as price and volume. For this purpose, use is made of various chart formations and statistical methods. It rests on the assumption that the formations arising on the charts because of past price movements constitute an indicator for the future, and hence that such formations can be used as an indicator for purchases and sales. Such analyses predict, for example, that the lowest prices realized in the past — in other words, the points at which prices began to rise — are 'support points', and hence these points should be awaited (be the criterion) for purchases, and again that the highest prices realized in the past are 'resistance' points and should be taken into account for sales.

Reverse RepoReverse Repurchase Agreement

Reverse repo transactions are generally carried out, in cases of a temporary liquidity surplus in the market, with the aim of withdrawing the excess liquidity. The central bank sells the instruments in its portfolio to the institutions authorized to conduct open market operations, with a commitment to buy them back at a future date (at the price determined during the transaction). The repurchase price is likewise determined on the date the sale transaction is made. The institution party to the transaction also undertakes to sell the instrument subject to the reverse repo transaction to the central bank at the transaction's maturity. From the central bank's perspective, the reverse repo transaction expresses the temporary withdrawal of liquidity from the market, within the framework of open market operations, for the duration of the transaction's maturity, in return for the instruments given.

Consumer Price Index - CPI Consumer Price Index – CPI

An index measuring changes in the prices of goods and services purchased by consumers. In calculating the CPI, first the expenditures made over a year on goods and services by a sample population representing the country as a whole are compiled. According to this expenditure structure, the relative weights of goods and services within the index are determined; goods and services consumed in large amounts by the sample population carry higher weights, while less-consumed ones carry lower weights. The prices of the determined goods and services are collected from all provinces on specific days of each month. The CPI is calculated using the collected prices and the weights set at the start of the year.
