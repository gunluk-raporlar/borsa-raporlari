Terms & Definitions

Ekonomi, finans ve borsa terminolojisi sözlüğünün A–B grubu. Tanımlar TCMB, Rekabet Kurumu ve iktisat sözlükleri gibi resmî kaynaklardan derlenmiştir. Diğer harf grupları:

A–B C–D E–F G–I J–M N–P Q–S T–Z

Abandonment

The transaction of the landowners, during the realization of the development plans, relinquishing the whole or a part of the real estate, with or without consideration, in conformity with the development plan, to the public benefit (road, green space, park, etc.). Contractor: the person who undertakes to carry out a work relating to construction and trade for someone else.

Absolute poverty absolute poverty

; The state of not having enough income to cover the most basic food, hygiene, health, and education services.

Absolute poverty rate absolute poverty rate

It is the ratio of the population living below the poverty line in a country to the total population.

Abuse of a Dominant Position

Undertakings holding significant market power can influence competitive conditions in the market more easily than their rivals. The abuse by dominant undertakings of this power in ways that restrict competition in the market is prohibited under Article 6 of the Competition Act (Law No. 4054). What matters in assessments under Article 6 is that a dominant position itself is not prohibited. An undertaking's market power may result from technological superiority, efficiency or a quality product/service.

Accelerator Program

The support program applied to accelerate the development of technological products and technology-intensive start-up (early-stage) ventures. Support is given on matters such as hardware and operational expenses for activities like expansion into international markets, increasing exports, and taking part in advanced entrepreneurship ecosystems. Grant Support: the support type given without repayment.

access to financial services access to financial services

It is the concept describing the extent to which economic agents can easily benefit from banking and insurance services.

Accountability Accountability

Means that central banks are accountable to society for the decisions they take, their consequences and their corporate governance. Accountability is a requirement of central bank independence and can be fulfilled in various ways. These are listed as the obligations set by law, internal and external audits, and the transparency and communication policies implemented by central banks.

Accounting profit accounting profit

It is the profit calculation that takes only explicit costs into account.

Accrued Interest Accrued Interest

The interest accumulated on an investment within the investment period, up to the payment date. For example, to purchase a government bond issued at 100 TL with a periodic 4% coupon payment every 6 months (182 days), the price to be paid 3 months later (91 days) is 102 TL, under the assumption that the market interest rate has not changed.

Acid-test ratio Quick ratio / Acid-test ratio

It is the liquidity ratio calculated by deducting inventories from current assets ((Current Assets - Inventories) / Short-term Liabilities); it measures the most liquid items.

Acquisition acquisition

It is the acquisition of control over a company by taking a majority or all of the shareholdings of another company.

active job search active job search

; activities such as applying to a job-finding institution or directly to an employer, searching for a job via the Internet or newspapers, taking oral or written job exams or interviews, preparing to start one's own business.

ad valorem tax ad valorem vergi

It is a tax levied on the monetary value of the object taxed.

adaptive expectations hypothesis the adaptive expectations hypothesis

It is the view that economic agents base their forecasts for the future on information from the past.

Adjustment for Seasonal and Calendar Effects Adjustment for Seasonal and Calendar Effects

The periodic movements repeating in the same periods of every year can be called seasonality. The calendar effect, on the other hand, includes the movements that can form due to effects such as the change in the number of working days in a period. In order to better evaluate the tendencies in the data and to compare consecutive periods, series are adjusted for seasonality and calendar effects by statistical methods.

Administered Price

They are prices determined by the enterprise or the public authority and which do not change in response to short-term fluctuations in supply/demand conditions. Some economists attribute this price stickiness to market power.

Administration Presidency)

A public organization with legal personality and a special budget, related to the Ministry of Industry and Technology, established with the aim of increasing the share and effectiveness of small and medium-sized enterprises in meeting the country's economic and social needs, raising their competitive strengths and levels, and realizing integration in industry in a manner consistent with economic developments. Credit: the provision or transfer of purchasing power to someone, on the condition of taking it back after a certain period.

Advance Advance Payment

It is a lending transaction against commercial paper and documents taken as collateral and government bonds and bonds registered on the exchange.

Example: The customer paid an advance of 10.000 before the equipment was delivered.(The customer paid a 10.000 advance before delivery of the equipment.)

İngilizce tanım: A payment made by a customer before goods or services are delivered, creating an obligation on the seller.

adverse selection adverse selection

It is the situation in which the party with less information about an economic activity is more likely than the other party to make the wrong choice.

aggregate demand toplam talep

Or abbreviated as AD, the total quantity of final goods and services that all units in the economy want to buy and are able to buy in a given period.

aggregate demand curve aggregate demand curve

A graphical tool showing the relationship between the general price level and the total production demanded, other things being equal.

aggregate supply toplam arz

Or abbreviated as AS, the total quantity of final goods and services that all units in the economy want to produce and are able to produce in a given period.

Agricultural Products Producer Price Index Producer Price Index for Agricultural Products

It is an index that measures price changes related to agricultural activity within the framework of agriculture, hunting, forestry and fishing.

Algorithmic Collusion

An algorithm is expressed as a path designed to reach a goal, a set of steps followed; in computer science it is defined as the method by which software performs a task. Algorithms can, for example, be used by undertakings as a price-setting tool in online shopping. An undertaking can monitor a rival's product prices through algorithms and set its own prices accordingly. At a more advanced level, algorithms cease to be a tool of competition

All else being equal all other things being equal

Or the Latin ceteris paribus, the assumption that all other effects are held constant when examining a particular economic relationship.

Allocation of Shared Costs

The allocation by a multi-product undertaking of its general expenses separately to the production costs of these products. While distributing general expenses among its products, the undertaking may take into account the demand elasticity of those products or the competitive constraints in the markets where they operate. For example, it may exclude general expenses from a product operating in a competitive market with high demand elasticity while keeping its price low in that market.

Allocative efficiency allocative efficiency

It is the production of goods and services in line with consumer preferences and demands.

Amnesty Plus

Under the additional leniency rule, those who cannot benefit from the leniency program in an investigation concerning one market may, if they apply with respect to a cartel in another market, benefit from immunity for the second cartel and receive a reduction in the fines arising from the first cartel. In Turkish competition law, the provision on this subject is made in Article 7(2) of the Fining Regulation.

Analytical Balance Sheet/CBRT Analytical Balance Sheet Analytical Balance Sheet/CBRT Analytical Balance Sheet

An analytical balance sheet is one in which the accounts on the asset and liability sides are netted against one another and consolidated under appropriate groupings. The CBRT Analytical Balance Sheet consolidates the items on the CBRT balance sheet taking into account the items relevant to the effective conduct of monetary policy, without detailed breakdowns. In line with the functioning of monetary policy, 'Foreign Assets', 'Domestic Assets' and 'Valuation Account' appear under 'Assets', while 'Total Foreign Currency Liabilities' and 'Central Bank Money' appear under 'Liabilities'. Anchor Nominal Anchor

It is used to express the magnitudes taken as reference in the economic decision-making processes. For example, the concept of the exchange rate anchor expresses that, following a central bank's making certain commitments for the exchange rate, and with the other economic units also trusting this commitment, the changes in the exchange rate are taken as the basis in the economic decisions taken, and the decisions such as price, wage increase and investment take shape within the framework of the exchange rate policy. In the absence of trust in such an anchor (policy), the selected magnitudes lose these properties. The monetary magnitudes, interest rates, inflation and growth rates are the magnitudes used as 'anchors'. Particularly in recent years, the difficulties arising in the use of magnitudes such as the exchange rate and the interest rate as anchors have generalized the use of inflation for this purpose.

Anchoring Effect Anchoring bias

It is the investor's tendency to rely too heavily on the first number they see (the purchase price, the peak price); it may overshadow current information about the target price.

Angel Investor

They are wealthy individuals who are described as a special type of investor making investments in the early stage of the enterprises and organisations they contain, which have high risk and high growth potential.

Annualization Annualisation

It is the annualisation of short-term returns or volatility; return is annualised with (1+r)^t−1 and volatility with σ√t (the square root rule).

Anti-Competitive Agreements

It refers to secret or open agreements between enterprises that restrict competition among them. Such agreements can be horizontal or vertical.

Appreciation appreciation

It is an increase in the value of an asset or a currency relative to other assets or currencies due to market conditions.

Arbitrage Arbitrage

The transaction of making a profit by simultaneously buying a security, currency or commodity in one market and selling it in another (exchanging the securities with one another). Arbitrage transactions lead to a reduction in price differences between markets. Today, with the growth of communication technology and the falling cost of accessing information, price differences between markets are extremely low, and any differences that appear are instantly eliminated by such transactions.

arc elasticity arc elasticity

It is an elasticity calculation method used to find the average elasticity between two points on a curve or line.

ascending price auction ascending-price auction

Or English auction, the type of auction in which participants submit open bids and the price is progressively raised from a low starting level until a winner is determined.

Asset / Liability Principle Asset/Liability Principle

The asset/liability principle is based on showing all loans and receivables from non-residents as gross assets and liabilities for stocks and by netting each category for flows.

Asset Backed Security Asset-Backed Security

The instruments of value that institutions can issue in return for the receivables that have arisen from their own commercial transactions. Consumer credits, housing credits and the receivables arising from export transactions are among the types of receivables that can be subject to this transaction. By selling asset-backed securities, banks can convert their receivables into cash beforehand.

Asset turnover Asset turnover

It is the ratio of sales to total assets (Sales / Total Assets); it measures how efficiently assets generate sales. This is the Turkish standard used instead of 'asset turnover ratio'.

assets assets

Association of Undertakings

Undertaking associations can from time to time direct their members through recommendation decisions in line with their founding purposes. Such recommendations may relate to sales prices and similar matters and may carry purposes and/or effects that restrict competition. It must be determined whether the recommendations of undertaking associations result directly in binding decisions of the association. Indeed, binding undertaking association decisions restrict competition

Asymmetric information asymmetric information

Auction Auction

In market economies, one of the methods of allocating various goods and financial values to individuals or institutions. In particular, if the good to be auctioned has no fixed or directly determinable price, or if the seller of the good is unsure about the good's market price, sale by auction eliminates this uncertainty under fully competitive conditions. Auctions can be conducted in different forms. The most common are the multiple-price (Dutch Auction) type auctions in which prices are ordered from largest to smallest, ensuring maximum revenue and minimum cost for the seller. In this auction, the transaction with each participant is made at the price it itself bid. Auctions in which prices are ordered from smallest to largest are called the 'English Auction'. Another auction method is the 'single price' auction. In this auction type, the price at which supply and demand are equated is applied to all the participants winning the auction. In other words, all the transactions are carried out at a single price. Both auction types have advantages and disadvantages relative to one another. In the single price auction, the cost of information gathering is lower and greater participation can be achieved. In the multiple price auction, more intense competition is in question and a lower cost may be in question for the seller. However, all these depend on the structure of the market and the conditions of the day.

Auction theory auction theory

It is the set of principles explaining how economic units behave under different conditions in transactions carried out under the best-offer method.

They are fiscal elements that operate automatically, quickly and effectively in establishing and maintaining economic equilibrium. Examples include progressive taxes and unemployment insurance payments.

Automatic stabilizers automatic stabiliser

They are elements of the public budget that come into operation automatically along with business cycles and have a dampening effect on fluctuations in the economy.

Base Effect Base Effect

Refers to the effect carried over to the period in which the change is calculated, when the reference period for the change contains a realization significantly lower or higher than normal. For example, in calculating the annual inflation rate for November 2014, the percentage change of the November 2014 consumer index relative to the November 2013 consumer index is taken. If the consumer index rose in November 2013 at a rate far below the normal average, a base effect raising the annual inflation in November 2014 will be observed.

Basel Committee on Banking Supervision Basel Committee on Banking Supervision – BCBS

An international organization operating under the umbrella of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) based in Basel, Switzerland, which prepares prudential regulatory frameworks for the banking sector on a global scale. The Committee also provides cooperation on the supervision of banks. With the aim of consolidating global financial stability, the Committee issues regulatory frameworks, particularly in the fields of capital and liquidity, and monitors the implementation of these regulations in countries. The Committee submits its fundamental decisions and work programme to the Group of Central Bank Governors and Heads of Supervision (GHOS) for approval.
