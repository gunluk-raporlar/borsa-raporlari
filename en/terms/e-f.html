E-Export

The fulfillment of the shipment as Micro Export, by issuing an invoice (VAT-free) on behalf of the individual customer for a foreign-addressed order received through the e-commerce/online (internet) sales channel. Economies of Scope

It is the existence of economies of scope if the cost of producing two products together is less than the total of the cost of a firm producing only product x and the cost of a firm producing only product y (competition terms glossary 102).

Exotic OptionsExotic Options

Options shaped according to the demander's needs. Examples of this type are options under the name 'barrier option' that lose their validity if, for instance, the price foreseen in the option for the underlying instrument is passed, or options under the name 'lookback options' that take, for example, the highest price realized in a certain period as the option's price.

EconomyEconomics

Or in other words economics, the branch of science that studies how individuals and societies use the limited resources at their disposal to satisfy their wide-ranging wants and needs.

Economic unitEconomic Unit

Economic fluctuationEconomic Fluctuation

Or business cycle; the rises and falls in total production, income and spending seen over multi-year periods in market economies.

Economic contractionEconomic Contraction

It is the period in which total output in an economy shows an overall decline.

Economic efficiencyEconomic Efficiency

It is a decision on which products are produced, how and for whom with limited natural resources, in a way that maximises social benefit.

Economic surplusEconomic Surplus

It is the total gain the market provides to buyers and sellers and equals the sum of consumer surplus and producer surplus.

Economic expansion Economic Expansion

It is the period in which total output in an economy shows an overall increase.

Economic Confidence Index Economic Confidence Index

An index summarizing consumers' and producers' assessments, expectations and tendencies regarding the general economic situation, formed by weighting and combining the consumer confidence indicator with the confidence indicators for manufacturing industry (real sector), services, retail trade and construction, adjusted for seasonal effects.

Economic profit Economic Profit

It is a profit calculation that takes into account both explicit and implicit costs.

Economic globalization economic globalisation

Electronic Fund Transfer System - EFTElectronic Funds Transfer System – EFT

The payment system providing the real-time electronic settlement of payment transactions made in Turkish lira. The owner and operator of the EFT system is the CBRT. The EFT System has two components: the Interbank TL Transfer System and the Customer Funds Transfer System. The EFT System operates between 08:00 and 17:30 every weekday except official holidays, and on half working days the system closes at 13:00.

Electronic Securities Transfer System - EMKT Electronic Securities Transfer System – ESTS

The securities settlement system enabling the transfer and settlement of securities among banks electronically, in real time and on a book-entry basis. In the EMKT System, transactions relating to government domestic debt securities and securities issued by some public institutions are carried out. In the transactions made in the EMKT System, the delivery versus payment (DVP) principle applies. Under this principle, the transfer of securities is made simultaneously with the realization of the fund transfer relating to the said securities. The EMKT System operates between 08:00 and 17:30 every weekday except official holidays, and on half working days the system closes at 13:00. The owner and operator of the EMKT System is the CBRT.

A monetary value issued in return for funds accepted by the electronic money issuing institution, stored electronically, used to carry out the payment transactions defined in Law No. 6493 on Payment and Securities Settlement Systems, Payment Services and Electronic Money Institutions, and accepted as a means of payment also by real and legal persons other than the electronic money issuing institution.

Enflasyon Inflation

It is a sustained rise in the general level of prices. Inflation is measured by official indices published by TUIK showing consumer and producer price developments.

Example: Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money. ( Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money.

English definition: A general rise in the price level.

Enflasyon Beklentileri Inflation Expectations

It refers to economic agents' expectations about inflation that has not yet materialised but will occur in the future. To measure inflation expectations, indices derived from various market instruments or surveys such as the Expectations Survey published by the CBRT may be used.

Enflasyon Hedefi Inflation Target

It is the annual CPI growth rate set by the TCMB and the Government for three-year periods. The inflation target is defined as the annual inflation in December of each year.

Enflasyon Hedeflemesi Inflation Targeting

Central banks' targeting, generally together with governments, of a specific inflation figure as the aim of monetary policy. In such a practice, it is envisaged that the targeted inflation, to the extent it can affect expectations, will serve as the nominal anchor. Its difference from other policies is the clear announcement of the inflation target to the public and the taking of direct responsibility in this matter. In practice, as the implementing institution, central banks acquire instrument independence directed toward the aim; in other words, central banks can use exchange rate and interest rate policies as they wish with the aim of bringing inflation under control. Such practices can also emerge as a necessity as a result of all the magnitudes in an economy losing their nominal anchor property. For the success of such a policy, elements such as a strong and healthy fiscal structure, an observable and stable relationship between inflation and monetary policy instruments, credibility, independence, accountability and transparency are counted as prerequisites.

Inflation Risk Premium Inflation Risk Premium

Refers to the risk premium added to the real interest rate to compensate for the loss that would occur if inflation materialized above the expected level. In other words, the nominal interest rate contains the real interest rate, inflation expectations and the inflation risk premium. This risk premium arises in countries where inflation fluctuations and uncertainties are present.

Inflation ForecastInflation Forecast

These are medium-term inflation forecasts updated every three months by the TCMB within the framework of existing data, models and analyses. While the inflation target announced for a given period is fixed, the inflation forecast is regularly reviewed in the light of new information.

Inflation CompensationInflation Compensation

It is equal to the sum of the inflation expectation and the inflation risk premium demanded by investors in order not to be exposed to real losses.

Inflation TaxInflation Tax

When money is considered a liability for the institution issuing it into circulation and an asset for the institutions and individuals demanding and holding it, inflation reduces the issuing institution's liability and the holding institution's asset in real terms. In this sense, inflation carries the property of a tax for the earner, and hence, like a tax, causes a transfer of purchasing power.

Inflation-Indexed BondInflation-Indexed Securities

Bonds whose principal and interest payments are linked to a certain price index and which therefore do not suffer a real loss of value. Because the real return of these bonds does not fluctuate despite changes in inflation, offering investors a predictable net return, these bonds are preferred by risk-averse investors.

Unemployment rate that does not raise inflation non-accelerating inflation rate of unemployment

Or, as it is commonly known, abbreviated as NAIRU, the unemployment rate that keeps the labour market in the country at long-run equilibrium and therefore does not lead to an increase in inflation.

Engel curve Engel curve

Or the income-consumption curve, a diagrammatic tool showing the relationship between the consumer's income and the quantity demanded of a particular good, with everything else held constant.

Engel's Law Engel's Law

Impact

Efficient Market HypothesisThe Efficient Market Hypothesis

This hypothesis predicts that the current prices of instruments traded in a market reflect all the obtainable information relating to those instruments. With the assumption that price is determined by buyers and sellers, the hypothesis assumes that traders can access all attainable information simultaneously and symmetrically. It is predicted that the price formed in this case is the equilibrium price.

The expression 'Euro' stems from the fact that the great majority of such deposits are held particularly at banks in Western European countries. US dollar accounts held in Asia and all other similar countries are also referred to by this name.

Explicit Collusion

It is an explicit settlement reached between competing undertakings on matters such as the determination of price and production quantity and the sharing of markets and customers.

Exploitative Practices

Some of the cases evaluated under Article 6 of the Competition Act are characterized as exploitative abuse. These are pricing or other practices causing a direct loss in consumer welfare. In such abuses, the dominant undertaking uses the advantage of its market power with the aim of extracting rent from consumers in a way a non-dominant undertaking could not. Excessive pricing and applying unfair trading conditions are among these cases.

Export Cartel

Undertakings operating in the same country acting together to sell or market their products in external markets, thereby obtaining an advantage against their international rivals. This can emerge in forms such as export prices being set jointly by rival undertakings, the sharing of customers, decisions on whether products are to be sold to certain regions via patent or license — that is, the conduct of an export restricted relative to what would occur in a free trade environment

Externalities, which are one of the market failures and create inefficiency, may not be reflected in the prices of the goods and services offered. Federal Funds RatesFederal Funds Rate

The money market interest rate at which financial institutions in the United States carry out their overnight borrowing. Since it is of the nature of a fundamental indicator in the implementation of monetary policy, it is under the close supervision of the US central bank (FED). With the aim of the daily 'Federal Funds Rates' forming in this market following a course consistent with its announced target rate, the FED adjusts the daily reserve (available funds) amount in the market through open market operations. For example, if the interest rates forming in this market are below the FED's target rate — in other words, if there is a fund surplus in the market — the FED withdraws this amount from the market through open market operations.

Federal Funds RateFed Funds Rate

The interest rate applied to overnight borrowings of the free reserves banks and other depository institutions keep in their accounts at the Fed.

Fiat money fiat money

Financial accountFinancial account

Financial Account - Balance of Payments DefinitionFinancial Account - on a Balance of Payments Basis

The growth of financial services enabling wider service options to reach every segment of society. The term 'financial deepening' can be used in a different sense in relation to the macro effects it brings to economies. Accordingly, greater financial deepening means an increase in the money supply, and the increase in the money supply brings economic growth along with it.

Financial StabilityFinancial Stability

Refers to the stability and resilience against shocks in the financial markets, in the institutions operating in these markets and in the payment systems. Stability in these areas generally brings with it the healthy and stable functioning of the financial system, and hence the productive allocation of the resources in the economy and the appropriate management and distribution of risks. It is a known fact that financial instability, on the other hand, will create serious problems in the economy, and the high cost of the financial crises experienced points to the importance of financial stability.

Financial Stability CommitteeFinancial Stability Committee

The committee established with the change made in Law No. 4059 by Decree-Law No. 637, which entered into force on 8 June 2011, in order to closely and effectively monitor macro financial risks in Türkiye. The Financial Stability Committee, under the chairmanship of the Minister of Treasury and Finance of the Republic of Türkiye, consists of the Governor of the CBRT, the Chairman of the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency, the Chairman of the Capital Markets Board and the President of the Savings Deposit Insurance Fund. Its fundamental duties are to control financial risks by providing coordination among the member institutions and to prevent the formation of systemic risk.

Financial Stability BoardFinancial Stability Board (FSB)

An international organization established in April 2009 as the successor of the Financial Stability Forum, with the aim of coordinating the work of national financial authorities and international standard-setting bodies, and of developing and implementing effective regulation, supervision and other financial sector policies, for the achievement of financial stability.

Financial leverageFinancial Leverage

It is the effect of debt financing on boosting return on owners' equity; since it also magnifies losses in low-profit periods, it cuts both ways.

It is used as an indicator of how much of the real sector's foreign-currency-linked liabilities it can cover with its foreign-currency or foreign-currency-indexed assets. Under the said report, financial market infrastructures are payment systems, securities settlement systems, central counterparties, central securities depositories and data repositories.
